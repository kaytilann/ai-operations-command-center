# Personas

## Persona 1: VP Operations

### Goals
- Hit OTIF and cost targets.
- Protect customer experience.
- Reduce firefighting.
- Communicate clearly with executives.

### Pain points
- Too many dashboards.
- Root-cause analysis takes too long.
- Teams debate whose data is correct.
- Financial impact is hard to quantify quickly.

### Key features
- Executive summary
- Incident prioritization
- Financial exposure model
- Scenario simulator

## Persona 2: Fulfillment Manager

### Goals
- Keep warehouse operations stable.
- Resolve bottlenecks quickly.
- Understand carrier and labor constraints.

### Pain points
- Alerts are noisy.
- Issues show up after SLA damage is already done.
- Manual coordination across systems is slow.

### Key features
- Fulfillment risk map
- Incident timeline
- Recommended operational actions
- Workflow handoff to Jira/Slack/email

## Persona 3: Inventory Planner

### Goals
- Maintain healthy inventory.
- Avoid stockouts and excess inventory.
- Balance service level and working capital.

### Pain points
- Forecast variance is hard to interpret.
- Promotions create demand spikes.
- Supply chain changes are not visible early enough.

### Key features
- AI-adjusted forecast
- Stockout risk detection
- Inventory exposure model
- Rebalancing recommendations

## Persona 4: Finance Business Partner

### Goals
- Quantify margin impact.
- Support tradeoff decisions.
- Reduce avoidable costs.

### Pain points
- Operational decisions happen without financial context.
- Savings claims are hard to validate.
- Cost-to-serve visibility is fragmented.

### Key features
- Cost/order trend
- Scenario simulator
- Margin impact estimate
- Working capital impact estimate
