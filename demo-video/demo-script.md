# Demo Video Script

## 60-second walkthrough

### Opening
This is the AI Operations Command Center, a concept product for fulfillment leaders who need to move from reactive dashboards to AI-assisted decision-making.

### Dashboard
The top layer shows executive KPIs: OTIF, orders at risk, inventory exposure, and cost per order.

### Incident intelligence
AI ranks incidents by customer impact, financial exposure, and operational urgency. Each recommendation includes confidence and modeled impact.

### Copilot
The user asks: Why are delays increasing in the Northeast?
The copilot synthesizes carrier SLA data, warehouse utilization, and order promise risk to explain the root cause.

### Scenario simulation
Before acting, the user models the tradeoff: rerouting premium orders protects revenue but increases Columbus labor utilization.

### Governance
The system keeps a human in the loop with an incident timeline and approval trail.

### Close
This product demonstrates how AI can turn fragmented operational data into faster, financially grounded decisions.
